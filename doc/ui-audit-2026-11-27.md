# UI audit, 27 November 2026

Scope: the board UI in a local sandbox (Northwind Robotics: 4 agents, 2
projects, 25 tasks across every status with sub-tasks, a thread with comments
and a plan document, a goal with two sub-goals, two pending approvals, one
routine). Every surface below was captured in dark and light at 1440 and 390
wide before any change.

Surfaces: Dashboard, Desk, Inbox, Tasks, a task page (thread, plan document,
side panel), a task tree, Agents, an agent page, Projects, a project (Tasks and
Deliverables), Messages, Approvals, an approval page, Routines, Goals, a goal,
Website home, Calendar, Deliverables, Settings (General, Members), the account
menu, Cmd+K (empty and typed), the new task dialog, an empty Tasks list, an
empty Goals page, the Tasks loading skeleton, a failed Tasks load, a task that
does not exist.

Screenshots: before and after pairs are in `doc/ui-audit-2026-11-27/`
(`-d` 1440 wide, `-m` 390 wide). The phone "before" shots still show the Add
to Home Screen card; it was dismissed for the "after" shots and is unchanged.

The rules from 22 October stand (solid focus ring, no opacity rows, toolbar
16px above its list, sections 24px apart, pages start at the left edge) and
nothing fixed then was redone.

## How it was measured

- **Contrast.** The 22 October probe, rebuilt: every element with its own
  text, its colour composited over its real background stack, WCAG ratio
  (4.5:1 body, 3:1 large). Each run plants #999 on #fff (2.85:1) and only
  counts if the plant is reported. The plant was caught on all 22 page runs that reported.
  Before: one failure, "Recommended action" on an approval in light mode at
  4.27:1. After: none on Approvals, an approval page, Inbox, Goals and
  Projects in both themes at 1440.
- **Phone overflow.** Page `scrollWidth` against the viewport on every
  capture (never over), plus the furthest right edge of anything inside the
  page. Approvals after the fix: 374px on a 390px screen. Putting the old
  header direction back in the live page moves it to 407px, so the check can
  go red.
- **Layout jump.** Position and width of the Agents copy button and the tab
  row before and after a click, at 1280, 1440 and 1728 wide in both themes:
  identical (181px wide, same row).
- **Scroll fade.** The rail's computed fade lengths at the top (0 and 32px),
  the middle (32 and 32), the end (32 and 0) and on a screen tall enough to
  fit everything (0 and 0).
- **Token gates.** A raw hex, a bracket class and a named colour planted in a
  component turned gates 1, 2, 3 and the named colour ratchet red; all clean
  without the plant.

## What was checked and found sound

Type scale and weights, the 24px page rhythm, toolbar gaps, hover and press
transitions (150ms, eased), the tab indicator, skeletons on every list page
(73 pages use `PageSkeleton`; none spin where the shape is known), empty
states with a next step (Tasks, Goals, Messages), the new task dialog, the
toast, focus rings, `text-wrap: balance` on headings, tabular numerals on
counts, 96px of scroll room under the Everest button, and reduced motion
guards. No page scrolls sideways at 390.

## Not verified

- A real phone. Everything at 390 is Chromium at that width, not Safari on
  an iPhone. The scroll fade needs CSS scroll timelines: Chrome, Edge and
  Safari 26 have them; older Safari and Firefox keep today's hard edge.
- The built bundle. The sandbox served the dev build; CI builds the bundle.
- Screens the seed cannot reach: a live run, a paused agent, budgets with
  spend, Deep Dive, Commerce, Clients, the org chart with more than four
  agents. Their progress bars moved to `--track` by the same one-word change
  but were not looked at.
