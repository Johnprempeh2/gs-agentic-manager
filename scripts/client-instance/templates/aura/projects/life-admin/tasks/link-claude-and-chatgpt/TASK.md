John cannot start any work until your own Claude subscription is linked. Do this first, then mark this task done.

1. Claude: open **Connectors** in the left menu, choose **Anthropic**, press **Connect** and keep **Claude Subscription** (the default). Sign in with your own Claude account.
2. ChatGPT: in **Connectors**, choose **OpenAI**, press **Connect** and pick **OpenAI Subscription**. Sign in with your own ChatGPT account.

Use your own accounts only. The Anthropic card talks about API keys; ignore that and keep "Claude Subscription".
