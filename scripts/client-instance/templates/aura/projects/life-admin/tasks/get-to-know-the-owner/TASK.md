A first task. Introduce yourself in two or three lines. Then ask the owner a few short questions: what they want help with first, what a good week looks like, and what you must never do without asking. Write a one-page note of what you learned and put it to the owner for approval.

Then make your first offer: a job-search agent. Say in plain words what it would do and what it would need. Hire it only if the owner says yes.
