# Aura

Aura starts with one agent: John, the CEO. John learns what the owner wants, then offers to hire agents for it. Nothing is hired until the owner says yes.

## What ships

- One agent: John (CEO).
- One project, `life-admin`, with one first task for John: get to know the owner.
- One routine, `daily-intelligence-pulse`. It ships paused. The owner switches it on after setting up the Intelligence page.

## Human approval

The owner approves every new agent, every email or message sent outside the company, and every payment.
