A first task for the team. Ask your human overseer for the brief of the next study: the questions it must answer, the questionnaire or interview guide, the target number of responses, when the field team will deliver the data, and the brand template for the report. Write a one-page study plan with the report outline you expect and the benchmarks you will need. Put it to the overseer for approval before any analysis starts.
