Gather the survey and interview data collected since the last cycle and check it for gaps. Plan this month's report with the `survey-to-report-outline` skill. Brief the Data Analyst and the Report Writer through subtasks, review the draft against the data, and put the finished report to your human overseer for approval. Do not send it anywhere yourself.

Schedule: 09:00 on the 1st of each month, UK time. The routine ships paused; the human overseer switches it on.
