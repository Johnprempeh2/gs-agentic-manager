description: Rolling project for survey and interview studies, from data checks and analysis to sector benchmarks and branded reports signed off by one human overseer.
owner: research-lead
---

Use this project for every study the team works on. The field team (people, not agents) collects survey and interview data and attaches it to a task here. The Research Lead checks it and plans the report, the Data Analyst builds the tables and benchmarks, and the Report Writer drafts the branded report. Nothing leaves the organisation until the human overseer approves it.
