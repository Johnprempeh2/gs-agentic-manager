Use this project for the content calendar and every piece of content. The Marketing Lead plans the week and writes the briefs, the Content Writer drafts, and the Social Media Coordinator adapts each piece for its channels. Every item waits in review until the human overseer approves it. Nothing is published, posted, scheduled or sent before that.
