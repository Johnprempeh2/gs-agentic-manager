Review every draft that is ready for review: check facts, brand voice and plain English against the brief. Send changes back to the writer, or add the draft to the batch for your human overseer. Put the batch to the overseer for approval in one request. Nothing is published, posted or scheduled until they say yes.

Schedule: 11:00 Monday to Friday, UK time. The routine ships paused; the human overseer switches it on.
