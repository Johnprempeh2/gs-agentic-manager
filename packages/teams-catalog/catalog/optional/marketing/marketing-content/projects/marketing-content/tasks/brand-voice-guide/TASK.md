A first task for the team. Ask your human overseer for the business's existing material: the website, recent posts, newsletters, brochures and any style guide. From it, write a one-page brand voice guide: who the business writes for, how it sounds, words to use and avoid, and three short before-and-after examples. Put the guide to the overseer for approval. Once approved, the whole team follows it.
