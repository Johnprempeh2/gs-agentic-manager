A first task for the team. Ask your human overseer, through the Marketing Lead, for analytics exports for the last three months of each channel the business uses. From them, write a one-page baseline: the normal reach, clicks and enquiries for each channel, the best pieces, and the numbers the overseer wants to track. Later reports compare against this baseline. Do not log in to any account; work only from the exports you are given.
