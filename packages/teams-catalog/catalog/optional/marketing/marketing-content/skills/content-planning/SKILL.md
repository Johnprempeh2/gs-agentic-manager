# Content Planning

Use this skill to plan or update the weekly content calendar. It follows the content calendar approach in the Content Machine team and adds a human approval step for every item.

## Steps

1. Start from the business goals for the month and anything the human overseer has asked for.
2. Look back at last week: what went out, what performed, and what was held and why.
3. Pick three to five topics for the week. Each topic must link to a goal.
4. For each item, record the topic, goal, channel, format, audience, owner, draft due date and planned publish date.
5. Set a status for each item. Status moves in this order only: idea, drafting, ready for review, approved, scheduled, published.
6. Only the human overseer can move an item to approved. Record who approved it and when.

## Rules

- No item moves to scheduled or published without the overseer's approval recorded on the task.
- If an item changes after approval, set it back to ready for review.
- Every claim, number and price needs a source. Customer names, logos and quotes need written permission.
- Write in British English and plain words.
