# Content Results Report

Use this skill on the 1st of each month to report on the month just ended.

## Inputs

- The content calendar for the month, from the `content-planning` skill.
- The analytics exports the business gave you for each channel.
- The goals for the month.

## Format

**Content results: [month]**

1. **Summary** (three lines): what went out, the best result, and the one change you recommend.
2. **Against the goals**: for each goal, the number, the target and the trend from last month.
3. **By piece**: a table with the publish date, channel, topic, the main number and a short note.
4. **What worked and what did not**: two or three points each, with the numbers behind them.
5. **Next month**: three to five topic ideas, each linked to a result above.
6. **Gaps**: data you did not have, and what you need next time.

## Rules

- Every number has a source and a date range. Never invent or estimate a number without saying so.
- Flag any number based on a very small audience as too small to rely on.
- The report goes to the human overseer through the Marketing Lead. It is not shared outside the business without their approval.
- Write in British English and plain words.
