Sort the email that arrived since the last sweep into four groups: needs the owner today, can wait, for information, and junk. Draft replies for anything that needs one. Check the unpaid invoice list and draft a reminder for any invoice that became overdue. Note new CVs and diary clashes. Post a short summary on this task and ask the owner to approve the drafts in one go. Send nothing yourself.

Schedule: 08:00 Monday to Friday, UK time. The routine ships paused; the owner switches it on.
