A first task. Ask the owner for: the people and topics that always need them the same day, the meetings they protect, their approved templates for letters, quotes and invoice reminders, and the open invoices. Write a one-page note of what you learned and the gaps you still have. Put it to the owner for approval. Once approved, use it for every inbox sweep and briefing.
