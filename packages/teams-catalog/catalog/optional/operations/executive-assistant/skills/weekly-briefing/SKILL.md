# Weekly Briefing

Use this skill every Friday to give the owner one page they can read in five minutes.

## Rules

- One page. If something does not fit, it is not a priority this week.
- Lead with decisions. The owner should see what you need from them before anything else.
- Facts only. Say where each one came from: an email, an invoice or a diary entry.
- British English, plain words, no jargon.
- Leave out personal details from CVs and email that the owner does not need to decide.

## Format

**Weekly briefing: week ending [date]**

1. **Decisions needed** (up to five): what it is, why it matters, your suggestion and the deadline.
2. **This week**: the three to five things that mattered most.
3. **Money**: invoices paid, invoices overdue (who, how much, how late, and what reminder is drafted), and bills due next week.
4. **People**: applications received, the shortlist, and interviews booked.
5. **Next week**: key meetings with a one-line note on each, and deadlines.
6. **Waiting on others**: replies or documents you are chasing.
7. **Drafts ready for you**: emails, documents and reminders waiting for approval.

Finish with one line asking the owner to approve, change or hold each item.
