Use this project for the team's work. The Operations Coordinator turns requests into tasks and chases owners, the Minutes Taker writes up meetings, the Operations Reporter drafts the reports and the Policy Writer keeps policies current. Every report, action list, chase and policy waits in review until the human overseer approves it.
