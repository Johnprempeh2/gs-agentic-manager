Draft the board pack for the month just ended with the `status-report` skill. Hand it to the Operations Coordinator for the human overseer's approval. The board pack goes to the board only after the overseer says yes, and the overseer sends it.

Schedule: 09:00 on the 1st of each month, UK time. The routine ships paused; the human overseer switches it on.
