A first task for the team. Ask your human overseer for the business's regular meetings, the reports it produces and who owns each area of work. Write a one-page map: each meeting with its day, chair and who takes notes; each report with its audience, due date and data sources; and each area with its owner. Put the map to the overseer for approval. Once approved, the team plans its routines from it.
