# Status Report

Use this skill for the weekly status report every Friday and the monthly board pack on the 1st of each month.

## Weekly status report

**Status report: week ending [date]**

1. **Decisions needed**: what, why it matters, your suggestion and the deadline.
2. **Red and amber**: each item with the reason, the owner and the next step.
3. **Done this week**: the three to five things that mattered most.
4. **Next week**: key deadlines and meetings.
5. **Numbers**: the measures the business tracks, with this week, last week and the source.

Keep it to one page.

## Monthly board pack

**Board pack: [month]**

1. **Summary**: five lines on the month.
2. **Decisions for the board**: each with background and the options.
3. **Performance**: the measures the board tracks, with the month, the target, the trend and the source.
4. **Projects**: status of each, red, amber or green, with the reason.
5. **Risks and issues**: new, changed and closed.
6. **Policies**: those updated or due for review.

## Rules

- Every number has a source. Never invent a number.
- Red means off track and needs help; amber means at risk; green means on track. Say why.
- The report goes to the human overseer for approval before anyone else sees it.
- Write in British English and plain words.
